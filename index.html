Questions

Short answers.

Will you repair the drywall and repaint afterward?

Our repiping service includes repairing the wall and ceiling openings made for our work, along with the associated painting. Your written proposal identifies the restoration areas and finish approach before work begins.

Will my home look the way it did before?

Our goal is to return the affected areas to their pre-project appearance. We review existing finishes and explain the restoration approach before starting. If a finish presents a matching challenge, we discuss the solution with you before proceeding.

What happens to the mess?

We keep materials organized, contain project debris, and clean as the job progresses. Project debris is removed as part of completion. Your home is not a dumping ground.

How do I know whether my home has polybutylene?

Start with identification — not the age of your home alone. An assessment considers accessible piping, available records, and previous replacements. Concealed areas may require further investigation.

What if some pipes have already been replaced?

Tell us what was replaced, where, and when. Share any available records. The proposal should distinguish remaining piping from work already completed.

Does repiping include drains?

Not automatically. This page focuses on water-supply piping. Your proposal will state whether drain work is included, excluded, or separately priced.
